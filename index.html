Mind Arrows - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: Mind Arrows is a privacy-first spatial puzzle game. We do not sell your personal data. Your game progress, level scores, brain training statistics, and player profile are stored strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Collect

Mind Arrows is designed to minimize data collection:

  • User Profile Data: When you set up your brain profile, you may optionally provide a display name and age. This data is stored entirely locally on your device within the app's SQLite database to calculate your cognitive training metrics and brain age index. We do not transmit this data to external servers.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Your completed levels, high scores, stars, unlocked arrow skins, and solution statistics are saved locally on your device.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Standard technical information such as device model, OS version, and non-identifiable crash diagnostics may be processed automatically by Google Play services to ensure app stability.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Mind Arrows integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve ads, including App Open ads, Interstitial ads, Banner ads, and Rewarded ads:

  • Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or ID for Advertisers) and device network information to serve relevant or test ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • You can learn more about how Google handles ad data by visiting: Google Ad Policies & Privacy.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Audio and Device Permissions

Mind Arrows respects your device security and requests minimal permissions:

  • Network & Internet: Used exclusively to fetch advertising content and check network state.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Used locally to provide tactile feedback during arrow launches and collision events.
  • Procedural Audio: Mind Arrows generates all music and sound effects synthetically on-device via PCM synthesis. We do not access your microphone, camera, or storage files.

4. Children's Privacy

Mind Arrows does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The game contains brain training logic puzzles suitable for all ages. Advertisements served adhere to Google Play Developer Family policy requirements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your gameplay progress and profile data are stored locally in your device's private app sandbox, you have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can reset all progress, stars, levels, and statistics directly in the app via Settings > Reset All Progress.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage from your device settings will permanently erase all local game data.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
