Privacy Policy

1. Local Storage Access

PulseTube requests access to your device's storage exclusively to scan, organize, and play your local audio files. We do not upload, share, or transmit your local files to any external servers.

2. YouTube API Services

PulseTube utilizes the official YouTube Data API to fetch search results, trending charts, and public playlists. By using these features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy (Google Privacy Policy).

3. Data Collection & Analytics

PulseTube is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers. Any playback history, favorites, or imported playlists are saved locally on your device via an offline database.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services like Google AdMob to display advertisements. These services may collect certain device information to provide relevant ads, subject to their own privacy policies.
